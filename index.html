Verified Zero-Cloud Security Shield

1. Complete Client-Side Isolation: Every calculation, video stream, cryptographic hash, and document render takes place inside your browser's V8 or JavaScriptCore runtime. Files are never transmitted to any central cloud server.

2. Hardware-Backed Encryption: The SHARLINE vault leverages the W3C Web Cryptography API. Keys are derived using PBKDF2 with 100,000 iterations of SHA-256 and authenticated using AES-256-GCM.

3. Transient Volatile Memory: Uploaded images, PDFs, and camera snapshots exist solely within your computer's RAM. Terminating the browser tab instantly dissolves all in-memory buffers.
